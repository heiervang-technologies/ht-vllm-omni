## PASCAL

**incompatible-because-bf16/fp8/FA2**

The restored Qwen file hardcodes `torch.bfloat16` and conditionally selects FlashAttention-2; both are dead on sm_61. The restored diffusion quantization package registers FP8 and describes BF16/FP16-to-FP8 conversion, also outside this fleet's usable path. The Pascal constraints require cu126-or-earlier, no bf16/fp8/tensor cores/FA2, and INT8 dp4a as the acceleration grain. Dead or obsolete files do not become acceptable merely because current registry wiring may not import them.
